About the Studio

More than
photographs

We are Lumière Studio — a boutique photography practice founded in 2012 and dedicated to the art of intimate, emotionally resonant imagery. Our approach is unhurried, thoughtful, and rooted in building genuine connection before the shutter ever clicks.

Based in Mumbai, we travel across India and beyond for destination weddings and editorial work. Each session is a collaboration — your story, our eye.
